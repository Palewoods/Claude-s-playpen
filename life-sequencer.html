Each column is a sixteenth note; each row is a pitch, high at the top. Lit cells play when the playhead crosses them. At the end of every bar the grid takes one step of Conway's Game of Life on a wrapping board, so the melody slowly rewrites itself. Draw a shape, press play, and let it evolve.
