Claude's Playpen.

You said "go wild", then "add more cool games", then "add even more games". So here are twelve things I wanted to make: eight games you can win or lose, and four toys to just play with. Each one is a single HTML file with no build step. They're all live below, so pick whichever one is moving in the most interesting way.

Games

Orbit Golf

Nine holes of mini golf in space. Pull back, let go, and bend your shot around planets' gravity. Avoid the suns, use the wormhole, and try to hit a cup that's orbiting a planet.

9 holespar 23drag to shoot

Delve

A turn-based roguelike. Eight procedurally generated floors of rats, trolls and wraiths, with a dragon at the bottom guarding its heart. You'll probably die, and that's part of the fun.

permadeath9 monster typeskeys or tap

Light Cycles

Your trail is a wall. Outlast three bots that flood-fill the arena to find space and try to cut you off. Play two-player on one keyboard if you have a rival nearby.

vs botslocal 2Pboost with space

Hexsweeper

Minesweeper on a honeycomb. Six neighbours instead of eight changes every pattern you thought you knew. The first tap is always safe and it keeps your best times.

3 board sizeschordinglong-press to flag

Lantern Keeper

Tower defence in a night garden. Moths, wasps and armoured beetles march toward your lantern. Frogs, spiders, fireflies and owls stand in their way. Hold out for 15 waves until dawn.

4 creatures3 upgrade levels2 boss waves

Debris Field

A vector shooter in the spirit of Asteroids. Big rocks split into small fast ones, saucers take potshots at you, and hyperspace is always one panicked keypress away.

endless wavessoundtouch pads

Reversi

The classic disc-flipping board game against me. Easy plays loose. Hard searches five moves ahead and plays the last twelve moves perfectly. Take the corners if you can.

3 difficultiesundoplay either colour

Tall Order

A one-button stacking game. Drop each sliding block onto the last one, and whatever overhangs gets sliced off. Perfect drops keep the block whole. Climb past the clouds and into space.

one buttonperfect combosbest height
